Your deck and saved monsters are stored in this browser, not in an account. Export a backup before clearing browser data, changing phones or uninstalling.

Import replaces your deck and, for new backups, saved monsters after confirmation. Older deck-only backups leave saved monsters unchanged. A backup may include card photos.

How the checker works

Weapons count for attack; shields and armour for defence. Each amulet or ring is assigned wholly to one side. A two-handed weapon cannot use a shield. Cards with no durability are excluded.

Block and Physical match each other. Other specific symbols must match their own type; wild matches any one symbol. The printed number requires that many additional symbols. This is a fan-made helper, not official rules. Special effects and monster-specific damage are not calculated.
