How the checker works

Weapons count for attack; shields and armour for defence. Each amulet or ring is assigned wholly to one side. A two-handed weapon cannot use a shield. Cards with no durability are excluded.

Specific symbols must match; wild matches any one symbol. The printed number requires that many additional symbols. This is a fan-made helper, not official rules. Special effects and monster-specific damage are not calculated.
